"True luxury is not merely seen; it is deeply felt. It is the harmony between heritage and contemporary refinement."

Catering to Sri Lanka's most discerning clientele, Interiors by Kavish Xavier redefines high-end living. Drawing inspiration from the rich legacy of tropical modernism, we seamlessly blend global contemporary aesthetics with local artisanal craftsmanship.

From opulent penthouses overlooking the Colombo skyline to serene sanctuaries nestled in Galle, our evocative dark palettes, rare woods, and bespoke brass detailing create environments that are unashamedly grand, yet intimately inviting.
